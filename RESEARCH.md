# Ingram Memorial Company — research notes

Demo for https://www.ingrammemorialco.com/ (Wetumpka, Alabama). This file records what the live site is, which peer sites informed the layout, and why the Google Reviews block is sample content.

## What the business is

Ingram Memorial Company is a monument and memorial company. The live pages describe monuments, installation, and maintenance, in granite, marble, and bronze, with a free consultation and an online catalog. It is not presented on its own pages as a funeral home. The keyword meta tag also lists funeral-home and pre-need phrases, plus the domain www.farmermemorialcompany.com. Those read as leftovers from the Design Mart / YOOtheme template, so they are not offered here as Ingram services.

## Original look

The live template is YOOtheme “yoo_air”: `style-green`, `menubar-glass`, `background-mountains`, `font-trebuchet`. Colors taken from `styles/green.css` and reused:

- Body green `#739A42`
- Header green `#507D32` (mountains header also uses `#82973b`)
- Link and heading olive `#57793D`
- Pale green `#D0E098`
- Deep green `#324E1F`

The referenced logo file `images/yootheme/logo_green.png` returns 404. The apple-touch icon is the Joomla mark, not a company logo, so this demo uses a wordmark (Ingram / Memorial Company) in those greens instead of inventing a pictorial logo. The homepage picture is a stock meadow with the Irish inscription burned in and a third-party watermark; it stays with that quotation. Monument photographs on the Granite page are the real pictures.

Farmer Memorial wording on the live site is treated as template leftover, not a second brand:

- About: “At Farmer Memorial Company we are proud…” is shown as Ingram Memorial Company
- Bronze: “Farmer Memorial Company offers bronze markers…” is shown as Ingram Memorial Company
- Image alts that said “Farmer Memorial” are replaced with descriptions of the pictures
- The Contact item that left the site for farmermemorialcompany.com is a local demo contact page

The catalog button on the Granite page is kept. It posts to the Design Mart catalog the same way the live page does (`catalogs.designmart.com`, destination `loving_memories`, dealer name Ingram Memorial Company).

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photographs, or branding were copied.

### 1. Parmer Monument — https://parmermonument.com/

A monument company serving West Georgia and East Alabama, so a close peer: granite, marble, and bronze, plus a free estimate.

Borrowed:

- Monument styles as a scannable set (flat, bevel, upright, slant, cremation, bench, bronze), each with a short explanation, instead of one long undifferentiated page.
- Materials called out as their own group. Here that group is only granite, marble, and bronze, which is what Ingram already says.
- A plain free-consultation action, which Ingram already offers.

Not borrowed: guarantees, “lowest prices,” cemetery-official endorsements, years in business, or their photography.

### 2. Richfield Monuments — https://richfieldmonuments.com/

Borrowed:

- An uncovered photograph with the headline and the consultation action in the band under the picture, not type set on top of the photo.
- A slim row of memorial-type chips instead of large padded cards.
- Reviews placed high on the homepage, under the opening statement.

Not borrowed: their testimonials, “since 1895,” process diagram, or pricing.

### 3. Sierra Headstone & Monument — https://www.sierraheadstone.com/

Borrowed:

- Numbered, ordered memorial styles so a family can compare flat, bevel, slant, upright, bench, and cremation forms. Ingram’s own list (family, companion, individual, half serpentine, wing, slant, bevel, flat, ledger, etching, bench, mausoleum, columbarium) follows that reading order.
- The catalog action sitting next to the phone number.
- Editorial serif headlines with small-cap section labels. Body type stays a plain sans, in the role Trebuchet played on the old site.

Not borrowed: published prices, warranty language, “47 years,” nationwide-shipping claims, or their gallery.

Not a verified rating or review count.” There is no numeric rating. “See all reviews on Google” opens a Google Maps search for Ingram Memorial Company Wetumpka.

## Left out on purpose

Prices, certifications, awards, staff lists, cemeteries served, warranties, equipment, a founding year, hours, and any service that appears only in the template keyword list (funeral home, mortuary, crematory, pre-need, pet monuments, porcelain portraits, glass etching, granite signs). The gallery stays empty of a work portfolio.
